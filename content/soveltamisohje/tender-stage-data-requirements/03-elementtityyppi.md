---
title: "Element type"
order: 3.2
section: "3.1.2"
---

The element type property makes it possible to identify the different element types. The data is entered when the tender calculation material is prepared, and it is updated and kept up to date at the execution stage as well, so that, for example, quantity take-off can be carried out in the final account of the contract using the property to group the elements.
