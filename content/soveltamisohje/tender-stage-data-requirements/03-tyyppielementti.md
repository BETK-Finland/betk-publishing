---
title: "Type element"
order: 3.7
section: "3.1.7"
---

Yes/No information on an element that has been detailed in the information model as a type element corresponding to the execution. A type element contains the reinforcement and the cast-in items modelled. Cast-in items are usually not modelled at the tender stage, so information on the cast-in items to be taken into account at the tender stage must be given in the other tender documents.
